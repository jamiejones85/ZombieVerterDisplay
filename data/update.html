⚠️ Important Warning

Do not disconnect power or close this page during the update process.

Interrupting the firmware update may brick your device and require manual recovery.

📋 ESP32 Display Update Instructions

  • Ensure your device is connected to power
  • Download the latest firmware (.bin file) from the releases page
  • Click "Choose File" below and select the firmware file
  • Click "Update" to begin the upload process
  • Wait for the update to complete (progress bar will reach 100%)
  • The device will automatically reboot when finished

📋 VCU Firmware Update Instructions

  • Ensure the VCU is powered and connected via CAN bus
  • Download the latest VCU firmware (.bin file)
  • Upload the firmware file using the form below
  • Click "Start Update" to flash the VCU
  • Wait for the update to complete - this may take several minutes
  • The VCU will automatically reboot when finished
